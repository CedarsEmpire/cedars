# Weekly Review Pack

## Overview
This is the weekly review pack for the 30-day internal pilot. Each week, the team reviews the evidence, resolves issues, and decides whether the pilot can continue.

- Public launch switch: OFF
- Internal pilot switch: ON for approved users only
- Review owner: Operations lead
- Final weekly sign-off: Founder / Super Admin + required stakeholders

### 3. Risk review
- [ ] No critical incident remains unresolved
- [ ] No unauthorized financial action occurred

### 4. Remediation review
- [ ] All critical issues have owners
- [ ] Remediation dates assigned
- [ ] Fixes reviewed and validated
- [ ] Follow-up actions scheduled

### 5. Decision for next week
- [ ] Continue without change

- [ ] Pause and fix before continuing
- [ ] Escalate to leadership
