# Pilot Kickoff and Launch Gate

## 1. Pilot kickoff statement
The CEDARS platform will enter a 30-day internal pilot with the public launch switch OFF.

The internal pilot switch is enabled for approved internal and trusted users only. The purpose of this phase is to test the real operating model, validate financial safeguards, confirm operational health, and identify all issues that must be corrected before any public or broader release.

This is a safety and assurance phase, not a public launch.

---

## 2. Pilot operating rules
- Public launch switch: OFF
- Internal pilot switch: ON for approved users only
- No public access during the 30-day pilot
- No broad or unvetted user access
- No automatic member balance crediting
- No public payout or settlement logic enabled
- Treasury observation remains read-only
- All system actions are logged and auditable
- Any material risk can trigger a pause or freeze

---

## 3. Pilot objectives
The pilot exists to confirm that the platform is healthy across the following areas:
- registration and access control
- dashboard accuracy and stability
- package flow testing across all relevant tiers
- financial and treasury guardrails
- AI worker and grid operational behavior
- risk visibility and incident handling
- audit completeness
- approval and governance integrity

---

## 4. Pilot execution phases

### Phase A: Setup and baseline verification
Days 1-3
- confirm internal pilot access
- verify roles and restrictions
- validate login, logout, and authorization
- verify dashboard integrity
- confirm audit logging and treasury observation status

### Phase B: Business flow testing
Days 4-14
- validate package selection across the tested ranges
- test financial guardrails and treasury review behavior
- verify dashboard state consistency
- validate user actions and approval paths

### Phase C: AI and operational workflow validation
Days 15-21
- test AI worker lifecycle and task flow
- validate grid actions and state changes
- confirm exception handling and risk escalation

### Phase D: Resilience and release-readiness review
Days 22-30
- review outstanding issues
- verify remediation evidence
- evaluate risk reduction and operational stability
- prepare launch gate recommendations

---

## 5. Launch gate conditions
The public launch switch must remain OFF until all of the following are true:

- [ ] the 30-day pilot is complete
- [ ] no unresolved critical defects remain
- [ ] all tested packages are validated
- [ ] treasury monitoring remains read-only and safe
- [ ] no unauthorized financial activity occurred
- [ ] all material actions are recorded in the audit trail
- [ ] support and ops teams are ready for live operations
- [ ] finance and compliance sign-off is complete
- [ ] governance review is complete
- [ ] Founder / Super Admin approves the launch gate

---

## 6. Launch gate decision
The final decision is made by:
- Product owner
- Engineering lead
- Operations lead
- Finance owner
- Compliance reviewer
- Founder / Super Admin

The decision can be:
- GO: public launch switch turned ON
- NO-GO: public launch switch remains OFF
- CONDITIONAL: remediation required before public launch

---

## 7. Emergency stop rule
If a material issue is discovered at any time, the platform may be paused or frozen by the operations lead or Founder / Super Admin.

This includes but is not limited to:
- unauthorized financial action
- critical service issue
- data mismatch or security breach
- audit log failure
- role or access control failure

---

## 8. Final policy
The 30-day pilot is the required proving ground. The public launch switch is not a default state; it is a controlled decision that occurs only after operational evidence and governance sign-off confirm the system is ready.

This pilot is the gateway to safety, trust, and long-term platform health.
