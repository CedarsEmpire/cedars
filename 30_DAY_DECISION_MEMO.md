## 4. Decision criteria
The following criteria must be met before the public launch switch can be turned ON:

### Required conditions
- [ ] No unresolved critical production risk remains
- [ ] No unauthorized financial action occurred
- [ ] Access control and role enforcement are working
- [ ] Dashboard state and values are accurate and stable
- [ ] Package flow is validated and safe across tested tiers
- [ ] Treasury observation remains controlled and read-only
- [ ] No automatic member balance credits occurred without approved logic
- [ ] No payouts were triggered outside approved review controls
- [ ] All material actions are recorded in the audit trail
- [ ] Finance, compliance, operations, and engineering have reviewed the evidence
