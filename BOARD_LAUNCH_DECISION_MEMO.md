# Board Launch Decision Memo

## Executive Summary
The CEDARS platform should not be opened to the public until a full 30-day internal pilot confirms operational health, security, financial safety, governance integrity, and user trust.

The public launch switch remains OFF during the internal pilot period. The internal pilot switch remains ON only for approved internal and vetted users.

This is a controlled assurance phase designed to validate whether the platform is safe and ready for broader release.

---

## Decision Principle
A public launch is not a default next step. It is a gated decision based on evidence.

The platform may proceed to public launch only after:
- the pilot is completed,
- critical issues are resolved,
- finance and compliance have reviewed the evidence,
- the operational team confirms platform health,
- the leadership board approves the launch decision.

---

## Why the pilot is required
The pilot is necessary because the platform includes:
- registration and access management
- package and value flows
- treasury observation and financial review
- AI worker and grid task operations
- audit and approval controls
- high-value operational and trust-sensitive actions

These elements require controlled testing before public exposure. Without a pilot, the platform risks exposing users to poorly tested financial, operational, or governance failures.

---

## Pilot Scope
The internal pilot will validate:
- user registration and access control
- dashboard accuracy and consistency
- package and value flow behavior across tested ranges
- treasury observation and financial guardrails
- AI worker and grid task execution under review
- audit completeness and traceability
- risk, freeze, and rollback capabilities

---

## Public Launch Rule
Public launch is not enabled during the pilot.

The public switch remains OFF until the final board review approves the launch gate.

This ensures:
- the community is not exposed before the system is proven
- review, governance, and compliance remain in control
- operational errors can be contained before public release

---

## Board Review Standard
The board should approve public launch only if all of the following are true:
- all critical pilot issues are resolved or explicitly accepted with mitigation
- no unauthorized financial activity occurred
- treasury observation remains controlled and non-productive
- no member balance is incorrectly credited or changed by uncontrolled logic
- workflow and audit integrity are validated
- support and operations are ready for broader user access
- leadership sign-off is complete

---

## Risk Statement
The platform presents operational and financial risk if launched before internal validation. The pilot reduces the risk of:
- data inconsistency
- unstable user access controls
- financial misprocessing
- unhealthy settlement behavior
- weak audit controls
- poor operational escalation preparedness

---

## Recommendation
The board should approve a controlled 30-day internal pilot with the public launch switch OFF. After the pilot, the board should review the evidence and decide whether the platform is ready for public release.

This approach protects users, preserves trust, and creates a disciplined foundation for long-term platform growth.
